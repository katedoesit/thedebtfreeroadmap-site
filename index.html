THE THINKING BEHIND IT

Debt payoff isn't a formula. It's a system that fits your life.

Most debt advice starts with a spreadsheet and assumes you're already organized, already motivated, already have $500 of "extra" sitting around. This roadmap starts somewhere more honest: exactly where you are right now.

01

See what you're actually up against

Before anything else, you need the full picture — every balance, every rate, every minimum, laid out in one place. No formulas, no guessing, no avoiding the number. Just the truth, made simple enough to look at.

02

Build the system that fits you, not a stranger's spreadsheet

Snowball or avalanche. Aggressive or steady. There's no single "right" way to pay off debt — only the way you'll actually stick with. This roadmap helps you choose the method and pace that match your goals, your income, and your life right now.

03

Move — one page, one task, at a time

You don't need a five-year plan you'll abandon by March. You need the next right step. Nine sections, one task each, in order — so you always know exactly what to do next, from where you are today to where you're trying to get.
